Cookie Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026 · Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what we store in your browser when you use linkloom.in, why we store it, and how you can manage or delete it.

1. What is a cookie?

A "cookie" is a small text file that a website stores in your browser. Linkloom also uses localStorage and sessionStorage, which work similarly but are stored locally on your device (not sent back to our servers on every request).

2. What we store and why

Strictly necessary (no consent required)

Not used: advertising, retargeting, or social media tracking cookies. We do not use Facebook Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag, or any advertising cookies.

3. How we implement analytics consent (Google Consent Mode v2)

All Linkloom pages load with Google Consent Mode v2 defaults set to denied for analytics and advertising. This means:

  • Google Tag Manager loads, but all analytics and advertising tags are blocked from firing.
  • No _ga or _ga_* cookies are created until you click "Accept analytics".
  • If you choose "Necessary only", Google only receives a "consent denied" signal — no behavioral data.

When you accept analytics, we call gtag('consent', 'update', {...}) with analytics_storage: 'granted', which allows GTM's GA4 tags to fire from that point forward in your session and future sessions.

4. Google Fonts

Our pages load fonts (Figtree, Faculty Glyphic, Mynerve) from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). This requires a request to Google's servers, which sends your IP address to Google. Google states that it does not use Font API data for advertising purposes and does not set cookies for font requests. If you prefer to avoid this, you can block fonts.googleapis.com in your browser's developer tools or via a browser extension.

5. Managing your consent

You can change your cookie preferences at any time:

Or clear Linkloom's localStorage manually:

  • Chrome / Edge: Settings → Privacy and security → Site settings → View permissions and data stored across sites → linkloom.in → Clear data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Manage Data → remove linkloom.in
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data → remove linkloom.in

Clearing site data will also sign you out of your Linkloom account.

6. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if we add new tracking technologies or change how we use existing ones. Changes will be communicated via the site notice system described in our Privacy Policy.
