Refund & Escrow Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026 · Last updated: 12 November 2026

Linkloom uses an escrow model: brand payments are held by Linkloom and released to the creator only when the work is approved. This policy explains how that money moves — and what happens when things don't go as planned.

1. How escrow works

  1. Brand accepts a bid. The brand reviews creator bids and accepts one. Chat does not open yet.
  2. Brand funds escrow. The brand pays the agreed bid amount to Linkloom's escrow holding. Only then does the creator see the deal as funded and can begin work.
  3. Creator delivers. The creator uploads the finished work (Reel, Post, Video, etc.) through Linkloom before the agreed deadline.
  4. Brand reviews. The brand has the opportunity to approve the work or request one revision (as permitted by the brief).
  5. Funds released. On approval, Linkloom releases the full agreed price to the creator's UPI or bank account, minus any applicable TDS as required by Indian tax law.

2. Platform fee

Linkloom charges brands and creators a subscription fee for platform access. There is no per-deal commission. The creator receives 100% of the agreed bid price (subject only to applicable tax deductions required by law, not by Linkloom).

3. When a brand can get a refund

SituationOutcomeTimeline
Brand cancels before funding escrow. The brand accepted a bid but has not yet paid into escrow. Full cancellation, no charge. Immediate; no payment was made.
Brand cancels within 24 hours of funding, before work starts. Full refund of the escrow amount, minus any payment processing fees charged to Linkloom by the payment gateway (typically 2–3%). 7–10 working days to the original payment source.
Creator fails to deliver by the agreed deadline. The creator did not upload the work and did not request an extension that the brand agreed to. Full refund of the escrow amount to the brand. Processed within 5 working days of the deadline passing.
Creator delivers work that materially does not meet the brief. Brand raises a dispute and Linkloom upholds it after review. Full or partial refund depending on Linkloom's determination. Up to 14 working days from dispute submission for Linkloom's review. Refund within 7–10 working days after determination.
Both parties mutually agree to cancel mid-deal. Funds split as agreed between creator and brand, or full refund to brand if no work was started. 7–10 working days after both parties confirm.
Note: If you cancel after the creator has started work (uploaded at least a draft), the creator may be entitled to a partial payment for work already done, at Linkloom's discretion on review of the submission.

4. When a creator is paid

  • The brand clicks "Approve" in the Linkloom platform. Funds are released immediately (processing time 1–3 working days depending on the payout method).
  • If the brand neither approves nor raises a dispute within 7 days of the creator uploading the final deliverable, funds are automatically released to the creator.

5. Disputes

If you believe the other party is not fulfilling their obligations, contact Linkloom at hello@linkloom.in with the subject line "Deal Dispute", including:

  • The deal ID or brief title
  • A description of the issue
  • Any evidence (screenshots, the delivered content, the original brief)

We will acknowledge within 24 hours and issue a determination within 14 working days. While a dispute is active, escrow funds are frozen — neither party can claim them.

Linkloom's determination about escrow release is final within the platform. This does not affect either party's right to pursue separate legal remedies under Indian law.

6. Subscription fees

Subscription fees paid to Linkloom are non-refundable, except where required by Indian consumer protection law or in the event of a billing error on our part.

7. Payment processing

Linkloom processes payments through licensed payment gateways. Linkloom itself does not hold your bank card details. Payout to creators is made via UPI or NEFT/IMPS bank transfer. Processing times:

  • UPI: typically same day (subject to bank processing)
  • Bank transfer: 1–3 working days

8. TDS and taxes

Linkloom may deduct Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) from creator payouts as required under the Income Tax Act 1961 (Section 194H or as applicable). The applicable TDS rate depends on your PAN status and income category. TDS certificates will be provided as required by law. Creators are responsible for filing their own income tax returns.

9. Contact for refund requests

Email hello@linkloom.in with subject "Refund Request". Please include your registered mobile number, deal details, and reason for the request. We will acknowledge within 24 hours.
