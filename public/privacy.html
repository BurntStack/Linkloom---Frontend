2. Personal data we collect

When you create an account (sign-up form):

  • Full name
  • Mobile number (OTP-verified once at signup; used to identify your account)
  • Password (stored as a one-way cryptographic hash — we never store your plain-text password)
  • Account role: creator or brand
  • Brand name (brands only)
  • Email address and profile picture (only if you sign in with Google — provided by Google)

When you use the platform:

  • Campaign briefs (brands), bid details and amounts (creators)
  • Job stage updates and messages between creator and brand
  • Payout details: UPI ID or bank account number and IFSC (held securely; never shown to the other party)
  • PAN or other tax identification if required by Indian tax law for payout processing
  • Profile information you add (niche, location, language, social media handles)

Automatically collected (with your consent for analytics):

  • IP address and approximate location (city-level)
  • Browser type and device type
  • Pages visited and navigation events (via Google Analytics / Google Tag Manager — only after you accept analytics cookies)

Browser storage (not sent to any server):

  • Your cookie consent choice (ll_consent in localStorage)
  • Which side of the site you last viewed (ll-side in sessionStorage)
  • Authentication session token (localStorage, only while signed in)

4. Third-party data processors

We use the following services. Each acts as a "Data Processor" on our behalf and is bound by their own data processing terms:

  • Vercel (hosting and CDN): Your requests pass through Vercel's infrastructure. They process IP addresses and serve the application. Vercel privacy policy →
  • Google Tag Manager + Google Analytics 4: Analytics only, and only after you accept analytics cookies. We use Google Consent Mode v2 — analytics tags are blocked until you give consent. If you choose "Necessary only", no analytics data is sent to Google. Google privacy policy →
  • Google Fonts: We load fonts from Google's CDN (fonts.googleapis.com), which sends your IP address to Google. No personal data other than IP is transferred. You can block this in your browser without affecting core functionality.
  • Google Sign-In: If you use "Continue with Google", Google provides your name, email and profile picture to Linkloom. This is subject to Google's own privacy terms, which you accept when using your Google account.
  • Supabase (database and authentication): Account data, campaign data and messages are stored on Supabase infrastructure (hosted on AWS, Singapore region). Supabase privacy policy →

We do not sell your personal data to any third party, ever.

5. Sensitive personal data

Your UPI ID, bank account number and PAN (if collected for tax purposes) are Sensitive Personal Data under India's SPDI Rules 2011. We handle these with additional safeguards:

  • Encrypted at rest and in transit (TLS 1.2+)
  • Never displayed to the other party in a deal — a brand never sees a creator's UPI ID, bank details or PAN
  • Accessible only to authorised payment processing systems; not accessible to general staff
  • Retained only as long as required by Indian tax law (typically 7 years for financial records)

6. Data retention

  • Account data: retained while your account is active, plus up to 3 years after account closure for dispute resolution and legal compliance
  • Financial and transaction records: 7 years, as required by Indian tax and accounting law
  • Chat messages and job data: retained for the lifetime of the account; deleted on account deletion except where needed for active disputes
  • Analytics data: as per Google Analytics retention settings (we set this to 2 months for user-level data)
  • OTPs: never stored after verification; expire automatically

7. Your rights under the DPDP Act 2023

As a Data Principal (the person whose data we process), you have the right to:

  • Access: request a summary of personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten"): request deletion of your personal data, subject to our legal retention obligations (financial records cannot be deleted early)
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw consent for analytics at any time by clicking Cookie settings in the site footer
  • Nominate: nominate another person to exercise your data rights on your behalf
  • Grievance redressal: file a complaint if we do not respond to your request within the required time

To exercise any right, email hello@linkloom.in with the subject line "Data Principal Request". We will acknowledge within 48 hours and respond within 15 working days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is constituted.
