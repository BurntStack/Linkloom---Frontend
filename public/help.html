🎨 For creators

How do I join Linkloom as a creator?

Go to linkloom.in/signin.html, choose "I'm a creator", enter your mobile number for OTP verification, then set a password. You can also sign in with Google. It's free and takes about a minute.

How does bidding work?

Brands post briefs describing the content they need — platform, format, deadline, and budget. You browse open briefs, pick one that suits your audience, and submit a bid with your proposed price and a note to the brand.

The brand reviews all bids and accepts one. If yours is chosen, the deal moves to the funded stage and you start work once the brand has paid into escrow.

Is there a platform fee for creators?

No platform fee for creators right now. The amount in the brief is what you bid on, and you receive the full agreed price (minus applicable tax such as TDS, which is handled per Indian law — not a Linkloom cut).

When and how do I get paid?

Once you submit the deliverable and the brand approves it, the escrow is released. Payment lands in your registered UPI ID or bank account within the payout processing time (typically 1–3 business days after release).

TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) may apply as per Indian income tax rules for influencer/digital marketing income. You will receive the agreed price minus applicable TDS; Linkloom does not take a separate cut.

What platforms and content formats are supported?

Currently supported platforms:

  • Instagram — Reels, Posts, Stories
  • YouTube — Videos, Shorts

More platforms (LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Moj, Josh) are coming soon.

Do I need KYC to get paid?

Yes. To receive payments you need to complete your payout details — UPI ID or bank account number and IFSC. Your PAN may be required for TDS compliance. KYC details are stored securely and are never shared with the brand.

What if the brand doesn't approve my work?

Brands can request revisions before approving. The brief specifies the number of revisions included. If a dispute arises and the work meets the agreed brief, raise it with our support team at support@linkloom.in — we review the brief, the deliverable, and the message thread.

If the work is never delivered, the escrow is returned to the brand.

Can I cancel a deal after it's funded?

Once you've accepted and the deal is funded, cancellation requires mutual agreement and is subject to the terms in the brief. If you cancel without delivering, the escrow may be returned to the brand. Repeated cancellations can affect your standing on the platform.

📢 For brands

How do I post a brief?

Sign in at linkloom.in/signin.html, create a brand account, then click "Post a brief". Fill in the platform, content format, brief description, budget, and deadline. Your brief goes live and creators start bidding.

How do I choose the right creator?

When bids come in you can see each creator's profile, past work links, and their bid amount with a note. You can message creators to ask questions before accepting a bid.

Accept the bid that best fits your brief — budget, style, and audience match.

Is there a platform fee for brands?

Linkloom is currently free to use for brands. The brief budget is the amount the creator receives (minus TDS as per Indian tax law). Any future fee changes will be communicated in advance.

How does the escrow payment work?

Once you accept a bid, you pay the agreed amount into Linkloom's escrow. The money is held by Linkloom — not released to the creator — until you approve the deliverable.

This protects you: you only pay when the work meets your brief. It also protects the creator: the money is committed before they start work.

What if I'm not happy with the deliverable?

You can request revisions — the number included is set in your brief. Describe clearly what needs to change. If after revisions the deliverable still doesn't meet the agreed brief, contact support@linkloom.in and we will review the case.

Approval is in your hands — the escrow is not released until you click Approve.

When do I need to approve the work?

Once the creator submits the deliverable, you have a review period to approve or request a revision. If you don't respond within the review window, our support team may step in. Timely review keeps the creator's income on schedule and builds a good working relationship.

Can I work with multiple creators at once?

Yes. You can run multiple active briefs simultaneously, each with a different creator. Each deal has its own escrow, timeline, and approval flow.

What data about creators can I see?

You can see a creator's public profile, platform handles, niche, past work links, and bid history on your briefs. You cannot see their UPI ID, bank details, PAN, or any KYC information — these are kept private and used only for payouts.

🔒 Escrow & payments

What exactly is escrow?

Escrow is a neutral holding account. When a brand funds a deal, the agreed amount goes into escrow — Linkloom holds it, not the creator, not the brand. The creator can start work knowing payment is committed. The brand can commission work knowing they don't pay until satisfied.

The money is released from escrow to the creator only after the brand approves the deliverable. If the work is never delivered, it's returned to the brand.

Is there a minimum or maximum deal size?

The payout calculator supports deals from ₹1,000 to ₹2,00,000. If you need a larger deal size, write to us at support@linkloom.in.

What happens to TDS on payments?

Under Indian tax law (Section 194R / 194H as applicable), TDS may be deducted on payments to creators for influencer marketing. Linkloom handles TDS deduction at source as required. The creator receives the agreed price minus applicable TDS. A TDS certificate (Form 16A) is provided for filing purposes.

For specific tax advice, consult a chartered accountant.

What payment methods are accepted?

Brands pay into escrow via the payment options available at checkout (UPI, net banking, debit/credit card). Creator payouts go to their registered UPI ID or bank account.

What if I need a refund as a brand?

If the creator doesn't deliver within the agreed timeline, the escrow amount is returned to you. For disputes about partial delivery or quality, contact support@linkloom.in. See the full Refunds & Escrow policy for timelines and conditions.

👤 Account & privacy

How do I update my profile?

Once signed in, go to the Profile or Settings section of your dashboard to update your display name, bio, platform links, and payout details.

Can I switch between creator and brand accounts?

Each account has a fixed role (creator or brand) chosen at sign-up. If you need both, sign up with a different email/mobile number for each role.

What data does Linkloom collect about me?

We collect only what's needed to run the marketplace: your name, mobile number (for verification), account role, and — for creators receiving payments — payout details (UPI or bank account). Analytics are only collected after you give consent via the cookie banner.

Read the full Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

To delete your account and all associated data, email support@linkloom.in with the subject "Delete my account" from your registered email or mobile. We process deletion requests within 30 days, as required by India's DPDP Act 2023.

Note: data relating to completed financial transactions may be retained as required by law.

How do I access or correct my personal data?

Under India's DPDP Act 2023, you have the right to access, correct, and erase your personal data. Email support@linkloom.in with your request and we will respond within 30 days.

How do I manage cookies?

When you first visit Linkloom, a cookie banner lets you choose "Necessary only" or "Accept analytics". You can change your choice at any time by visiting the Cookie settings page.
